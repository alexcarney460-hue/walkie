Rental compute

Owners of a team can rent machines that RC Studios runs for the team ("Add compute" in the dashboard, or walkie compute). Rented machines run in RC Studios' account with DigitalOcean, LLC, our infrastructure provider, and join your team as the renting owner's machines. You are responsible for what your team's agents do on them, as on your own machines.

Rentals are paid from prepaid compute credit, bought in blocks through Stripe Checkout. Credit is used per started minute at the hourly price shown when you rent, and a machine needs its first hour covered by credit that stays unexpired through that hour: credit that expires within the hour does not count towards starting a machine. This also applies when a machine waits for provider capacity: it is not created if its credit would expire within its first hour. Each machine includes the outbound data allowance shown with the prices; data beyond it is charged per GiB. Prices are shown in the dashboard and by walkie compute quotes. A machine stops when the credit runs out, when it stays idle for its idle setting, when it stops reporting, or at its outbound data cap.

Compute credit is non-refundable. Each block expires 60 days (60 × 24 hours) after it is credited to your account, which is normally the moment you pay. Any part of a block still unused at that moment is written off and cannot be restored or refunded. When you hold several blocks, the credit that expires soonest is always used first.

If credit expires while a machine is running and no other unexpired credit covers it, the machine is stopped exactly as when credit runs out, and the machine and its disk are deleted. Machines waiting in the queue whose first hour is no longer covered by credit that lasts through it are cancelled. This can happen up to an hour before a block expires.

Credit expires on its schedule whatever the state of the service: while a compute account is frozen after a payment dispute, refund or fraud warning, and while rentals are paused or unavailable, including during our own maintenance. Nothing pauses or extends it.

You can see each block's remaining amount and its expiry date (to the minute, in UTC) in the dashboard under Add compute (or under Compute credit while rentals are unavailable), and with walkie compute credit; blocks that expire within 7 days are highlighted. Stripe Checkout states these terms before you pay.

Boot-failure credit. If a rented machine fails to boot, the charge for its first 15 minutes is credited back. A boot counts as failed when the machine never reports in and never joins your team: either it sends no report within 15 minutes of the provider starting it, or it reports that its own setup failed before it ever reported in. The credit is applied once DigitalOcean confirms the machine is deleted. At most 3 failed boots per team in any rolling 24 hours are credited; later ones stay charged. Minutes after the first 15, including the time DigitalOcean takes to delete the failed machine, are billed as usual.

Billing corrections. If DigitalOcean deleted a machine earlier than our metering recorded (for example when our own clean-up removed it first), the minutes we billed after the actual deletion are corrected and credited back. That is a correction of our metering, not a refund.

Credit that is credited back, for a failed boot or a correction, returns to the block it was paid from and keeps that block's expiry date; credit returned to a block that has already expired is written off.

Everything on a rented machine is deleted when it stops: its disk is deleted with the machine, and there are no snapshots or backups. Do not keep the only copy of anything on a rented machine: machines also stop on their own, as described above. Billing continues until DigitalOcean confirms the deletion. After you stop a machine, the deletion usually takes a minute or two; when deletions are delayed it can take about 50 to 80 minutes, and that time is charged to your credit. Time caused by an outage of our own service is not charged.

You must not use rented machines for cryptocurrency mining, or for anything else DigitalOcean's Acceptable Use Policy prohibits (https://www.digitalocean.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy). On suspected abuse we may stop the machines, put the compute account under review, and suspend or end your access to rental compute. The minutes used are charged and not refunded, and data on a stopped machine is deleted.

A dispute, refund or fraud warning on a credit purchase freezes the compute account and stops its machines. Capacity is limited: a rental that does not fit yet is queued and starts when capacity frees, and a rental the provider cannot start ends without charge. Rental compute is provided as available, like the rest of the service.
